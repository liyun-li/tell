How it works

TELL. mixes six games into one. Every round hides a tell: spot it. Each round is worth up to 3 points.

  • Daily Mix: one round from each game, in a new order every day, the same for everyone. New mix at midnight (Taipei).
  • Shuffle: endless random rounds that get harder as you go. A miss costs a life; you have 3.
  • Tiles: play just one game, same rules as Shuffle.

  • 🔪 Crime Scene or Cozy Studio?
  • 📐 Who Lives Like This?

  • 🧾 Paper Trail

Rules we keep

  • Real cases come from public records: final court rulings, DOJ press releases and the FBI's public lists, each linked on its reveal.
  • Missing-person cases are never scored.
  • No gore, and no jokes about race, gender, religion, disability or looks.
  • Your browser loads only the games' own cached copies. It never contacts the FBI, DOJ or court sites unless you tap a source link.

Your scores and streak stay in your browser. No accounts, no tracking.

怎麼玩

「破綻」把六款遊戲混在一起，每一題都藏著破綻，看你抓不抓得到。每回合最多 3 分。

  • 每日挑戰：每款遊戲各一題，順序每天換，大家都一樣。台灣時間午夜換新。
  • 隨機混搭：無盡隨機出題，越玩越難。答錯扣一條命，總共三條。
  • 單款遊戲：只玩一款，規則跟隨機混搭一樣。

我們的原則

  • 真實案件都來自公開紀錄：判決確定的裁判書、美國司法部新聞稿、FBI 公開名單，揭曉時都附上來源。
  • 失蹤人口案件絕不計分。
  • 不寫血腥細節，不拿種族、性別、宗教、身心障礙或外表開玩笑。
  • 瀏覽器只讀取各遊戲自己的快取資料，除非你點開來源連結，否則不會連到 FBI、司法部或法院網站。

分數和連續天數只存在你的瀏覽器裡。不用註冊，也不追蹤。
